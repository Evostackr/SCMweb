Privacy & Security Transparency

Zero Hidden Permissions. Total Transparency.

SMSCampaignManager only requests permissions directly essential to performing its core SMS dispatch and scheduling features.

Android Permission Purpose & Usage Required?
android.permission.SEND_SMS Enables the device to transmit SMS messages directly through the selected carrier SIM card. Mandatory
android.permission.READ_PHONE_STATE Inspects active SIM subscriptions (Slot 1 vs Slot 2), carrier network names, and roaming status. Mandatory
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays persistent foreground service progress and dispatch completion alerts on Android 13+. Mandatory
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE Ensures background SMS sending operations continue without being killed by OS battery optimization. Mandatory
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores scheduled campaigns and alarms automatically if your device restarts. Optional / Background
android.permission.READ_CONTACTS Allows importing contacts directly from the device's native address book. Optional
