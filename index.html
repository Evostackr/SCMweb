Enterprise Capabilities

Engineered For High-Scale Local Delivery

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room SQLite, and Android system services for high throughput, battery resilience, and precision delivery tracking.

Native Dual-SIM Balancing

Direct integration with Android SubscriptionManager. Detects active SIM slots (Jio, Airtel, Vodafone, T-Mobile), monitors roaming, and alternates automatically.

Smart CSV Ingestion

Sanitizes phone numbers on the fly, strips whitespace, standardizes country codes to E.164 (+91, +1), and purges duplicate numbers instantly.

Dynamic Tag Studio

Personalize each text with {name}, {code}, or custom CSV column tags. Real-time GSM-7 vs UCS-2 Unicode segment calculator prevents billing surprises.

Persistent Foreground Service

Runs as an unstoppable Android Foreground Service with CPU WakeLocks. Continues sending smoothly even when your phone screen turns off or switches apps.

Auto-Resume & Quota Resets

Pauses gracefully when carrier daily limits hit, then automatically resumes via MidnightResetReceiver at 00:00. Survives unexpected device reboots.

AES-256 Offline Security

Customer phonebooks stay strictly on your device. Encrypted local Room SQLite storage, zero analytics tracking, and 1-tap PDF & CSV delivery audit report exports.

Why Device-Based Sending Wins

Compare traditional cloud SMS vendors with your own smartphone running SMS Campaign Manager.

Cloud SMS Gateways (Twilio, Sinch, etc.)

High Costs & Data Exposure

  • ❌ $0.015 to $0.05 per SMS: Sending 10,000 texts costs $150 to $500 every single campaign.
  • ❌ Customer Privacy Risks: Your entire phonebook is uploaded to third-party databases.
  • ❌ Vendor Lock-in: Lengthy account verifications, complex DLT paperwork, and sudden vendor account freezes.
  • ❌ Recurring Subscriptions: Ongoing monthly platform retainers just to maintain access.
SMS Campaign Manager (Evostackr)

Direct-to-Carrier Autonomy

  • ✅ Zero Cost Per SMS: Uses existing carrier SIM plans or prepaid unlimited packs already on your phone.
  • ✅ 100% Offline & Private: Contact lists and messages stay encrypted locally in Room SQLite.
  • ✅ Dual-SIM Support: Auto-switch or balance campaigns between SIM 1 and SIM 2.
  • ✅ Unstoppable Foreground Engine: Keeps sending even when the screen is locked or phone reboots.
