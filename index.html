Enterprise Capabilities

Engineered For High-Scale Local Delivery

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room SQLite, and Android system services for high throughput, battery resilience, and precision delivery tracking.

Native Dual-SIM Balancing

Direct integration with Android SubscriptionManager. Detects active SIM slots (Jio, Airtel, Vodafone, T-Mobile), monitors roaming, and alternates automatically.

Bulk CSV Deduplication

High-speed contact parser. Handles 50,000+ rows instantly, normalizes E.164 phone formats, flags malformed numbers, and purges duplicates before dispatching.

Anti-Spam Jitter Delays

Customizable dispatch intervals (3 to 10 seconds) with randomized micro-jitter. Emulates authentic human sending rhythms to safeguard SIM cards against carrier throttling.

Persistent Foreground Engine

Background execution powered by an Android Foreground Service with partial CPU WakeLocks. Dispatches continuously without interruption even when the screen is locked.

100% Offline & AES-256 Storage

Zero cloud tracking, zero telemetry leaks, and zero remote data storage. Contacts and message records reside strictly on-device inside an encrypted Room SQLite database.

Delivery Audits & PDF Export

Real-time telemetry tracking SENT, DELIVERED, and FAILED states via Android PendingIntent callbacks. Generate professional PDF audit reports with a single tap.
